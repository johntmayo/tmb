# TMB photo hit list

Drop replacement photos in this folder using the exact filenames below. The app already points to them and falls back to the current generic alpine images until each file arrives.

Use landscape images when possible. From Wikimedia Commons, choose **1,280 × 960**; it is sharp enough for high-density phones without bloating the offline app. Phone photos around 1,200–1,600 px wide are also ideal. JPG or WebP content works, but keep the `.jpg` filename shown here.

For every Wikimedia image, retain the source URL, creator, and license so the app can display proper attribution.

## Priority 1 — the six hike cards

1. `col-du-tricot.jpg`
   - Search: “Col du Tricot Tour du Mont Blanc view”
   - Best shot: recognizable pass or hikers on the approach, with the Miage side visible.

2. `col-du-bonhomme.jpg`
   - Search: “Col du Bonhomme TMB trail September”
   - Best shot: broad rocky pass, TMB signs, or the traverse toward Croix du Bonhomme.

3. `col-de-la-seigne.jpg`
   - Search: “Col de la Seigne France Italy border Mont Blanc”
   - Best shot: border marker with the Italian Val Veny view.

4. `grand-col-ferret.jpg`
   - Search: “Grand Col Ferret Tour du Mont Blanc”
   - Best shot: pass marker or the view down into the Swiss Val Ferret.

5. `alp-bovine.jpg`
   - Search: “Alpage de Bovine TMB view”
   - Best shot: pasture, stone buildings, cows, or the Rhône valley overlook.

6. `aiguillette-des-posettes.jpg`
   - Search: “Aiguillette des Posettes ridge Mont Blanc view”
   - Best shot: the ridgeline with Mont Blanc or the Chamonix valley behind it.

## Priority 2 — arrivals and cities

7. `les-houches.jpg`
   - Search: “Les Houches village Mont Blanc”
   - Best shot: village and mountain context rather than a hotel exterior.

8. `lyon-vieux-lyon.jpg`
   - Search: “Vieux Lyon street traboule”
   - Best shot: warm evening street, Renaissance façades, or a traboule.

9. `lyon-croix-rousse.jpg`
   - Search: “Croix Rousse Lyon view”
   - Best shot: hillside city view, mural, market, or café scene.

10. `paris-arrival.jpg`
    - Search: “Paris Gare de Lyon clock tower”
    - Best shot: station clock tower or a first-arrival Paris street.

11. `paris-la-cigale.jpg`
    - Search: “La Cigale Paris exterior”
    - Best shot: the venue marquee or Pigalle at blue hour.

12. `paris-streets.jpg`
    - Search: “Paris neighborhood street morning”
    - Best shot: an intimate street or café, not a generic Eiffel Tower postcard.

13. `paris-sunday.jpg`
    - Search: “Paris Seine Sunday autumn”
    - Best shot: Seine walk, park, or relaxed Sunday scene.

14. `paris-departure.jpg`
    - Search: “Paris rooftops sunrise”
    - Best shot: a quiet final-look image that reads as departure.

15. `flight-to-geneva.jpg`
    - Best shot: your own wing or airport photo. A generic Alps-from-the-air image also works.
