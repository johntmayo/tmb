# Route data sources

The interactive map combines named markers and transfer lines from the supplied UMAP backup with trimmed GPX tracks.

## GPX tracks used

- Hike 1: `v1a-b_les_houches-contamines__via_miage_.gpx`, extended to Gîte Le Pontet with the opening of Stage 2
- Hike 2: `2_les_contamines-les_chapieux.gpx`, trimmed to start at Gîte Le Pontet
- Hike 3: merged portions of `3_les_chapieux-elisabetta.gpx`, `4_elisabetta-courmayeur.gpx`, and `v4b_combal_to_courmayeur__via_val_veny_.gpx`
- Hike 4: `6_bonatti_to_la_fouly.gpx`, trimmed to start at Arnuva
- Hike 5: `8_champex-trient.gpx` via Alp Bovine
- Hike 6: `9_trient_to_tre-le-champs.gpx`, followed by the short walk to Montroc-le-Planet station

The Fenêtre d’Arpette, Col des Fours, Le Tour, Croix de Fer, and other supplied variants are intentionally excluded because they do not match the final itinerary.

GPX route geometry is for trip planning and overview only. Carry a downloaded offline hiking map and follow current trail signs and local conditions.
